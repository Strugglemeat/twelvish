now you can, at any time, spend your "points" on three different things:
1) if an opponent is attacking you, you can use your points to offset theirs (ala puyo, spf2t) - garbage attacks don't fall until the attacked player has set their piece down
2) you can send the equivalent garbage amount to the amount of points you have
3) you can choose from a special attack which is inflicted on your opponent immediately (not delayed until they dropped their current piece)
these special attacks are chosen at character select, and you cycle through them by pressing UP


SWAP
switch your opponent's next piece with yours
~~~~~~~~~~~
DESTROY
destroy your opponent's current piece
~~~~~~~~~~
BLEAK
make your opponent's next piece queue invisible for X turns
~~~~~~~~~~
LOCKED
opponent can't rotate for X turns
~~~~~~~~~
MIXED
reverse opponent inputs for X turns
~~~~~
TOWERS
fill opponent's columns 1 and 7 with garbage
~~~~~~
SHIELD
your opponent can't use spells on you for X turns
~~~~~~~
FROZEN
your opponent can't make matches for X turns (low #)
~~~~~~
RISE
raises the opponent's field by one
~~~~~~
DROPPY
lowers your field by one

*hard drop

*extra spell slot
*can't cycle, only rotate
*can't rotate, only cycle
*can't rotate or cycle
*hold piece
*skip piece
*2 colors to match
